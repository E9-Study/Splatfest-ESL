📜 Anarchy Battle: Official Rulebook

🗣️ Global System: ESL Curtain & Dual Timers

When ESL Mode is active, an opaque curtain covers the board at the start of each turn while clocks pause. Teams have a 20-second Reading Timer ("Burning Fuse") to answer an A1–A2 question. Every wrong choice deducts 3 seconds from their upcoming 15-second Action Timer (minimum 3s). If the 20s Reading Timer runs out, the turn is forfeited and triggers an auto-step!

🌧️ Game Mode 1: Rainmaker (2-Team Race)

  • Setup: 9x9 grid, 10 Barricades per team, 5-minute chess clock per team.
  • Turn Actions: Move your pawn 1 square orthogonally OR place a 2-square Barricade between cells.
  • The Golden Rule: Barricades cannot overlap, cross, or completely seal off any player from their goal row.
  • Jumping: Face-to-face pawns can jump straight over an opponent (or diagonally if a wall or edge sits directly behind them).
  • Win: First team to reach any square on the opposite starting row, or if the opponent's 5-minute clock expires.

👑 Game Mode 2: Quad Color Battle (4-Team Turf War)

  • Setup: 9x9 grid, 5 Barricades per team, 10-minute global clock, 15-second turn clock.
  • Roles & Victory:
    • Attackers (2 Teams): Win together if Combined Attacker Turf > Defender Turf (and beats Mercenary).
    • Defender (1 Team): Wins if Defender Turf > Combined Attacker Turf (and beats Mercenary).
    • Mercenary "The Saboteur" (1 Team): Wins if their Individual Turf beats both Combined Attackers and Defender!
  • The Crown & Sprinklers: An Attacker on or adjacent to the center Crown (4,4) can spend 1 turn Channeling it to place a Sprinkler Token on their half of the board. Sprinklers automatically claim a 3x3 turf grid at the end of every round (every 4 turns). Max 2 captures.
  • ⚡ The Saboteur Mechanic: If the Mercenary steps onto a square with an enemy Sprinkler, they instantly HACK it—flipping the Sprinkler and its entire 3x3 turf grid to the Mercenary's color permanently!

🦑 Game Mode 3: Splatfest (ESL Quiz Turf Gauntlet)

  • Setup & Drafting: Choose 2 Teams or 3 Teams, and Easy (Pre-A1–A1), Normal (A1–A2), or Hard (A1–B1) difficulty. Spin the roulette to draft themes (with unlimited Theme Rerolls) and receive random pastel team colors.
  • 10x10 Grid & Hidden Points: Standard play lasts 3:00. Each question carries a secret point value scaled by CEFR difficulty.
    • Phase 1 (0–29 Points): Squares fill sequentially from your team's edge inward toward the center.
    • Phase 2 (30+ Points — Shared Trigger!): Once any team reaches 30 points, Phase 2 activates for all teams! New squares spawn randomly anywhere on the 10x10 grid, allowing teams to override and steal enemy squares!
  • Overtime & The Blind Round (0:30): When the 3:00 timer expires, a 30-second Overtime starts and a giant Color Splash covers the 10x10 grid so nobody can see the score while teams make their final pushes!
  • The Reveal: At 0:00, a drumroll plays while the percentage bar undulates back and forth before locking in the final percentages out of 100% with a punch!
